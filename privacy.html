ShiftSketch: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

ShiftSketch is a roster proposal tool that works entirely on your device.

  • We collect nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network connections.
  • Your data stays on your device. Rosters, shift times, staff names and notes you enter are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. We cannot see them.
  • Sharing is your choice. When you export or print a roster, the file goes to the app you pick in the Android share or print screen. We do not receive it.
  • Deleting your data. Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app and the data is removed.
